## About Me

I am a first-year PhD candidate at [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/), co-advised by [Prof. Junxian He](https://sites.google.com/view/junxian-he). I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/) (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. Specifically, I am interested in:
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
